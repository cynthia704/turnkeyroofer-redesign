What a Roof Replacement Looks Like Stage by Stage

Three stages, in order. Ask to see each one on your own roof.

STAGE 01

Strip and Inspect

The old roof comes off and the decking underneath is exposed. This is where rot and hidden damage get found, and where you get shown before anything is covered.

STAGE 02

Dry-In

Underlayment goes down. This is the layer that does the work when wind lifts a shingle, and it is the reason a roof survives the next named storm.

STAGE 03

Finish and Clean

Shingle, metal, slate or tile goes on. Ventilation is set to the attic. Once payment is received, the project is completed within 24 hours.

Here is what we can promise about the price. The estimate is free. The estimate is the price. If we find rot underneath, we show you before we touch it, and you decide. And if the number is more than you want to spend at once, ask about financing.

What moves the number

  1. Size and pitchA steep roof takes longer and needs more safety rigging than a low one of the same size.
  2. The materialShingle, metal, slate and tile are not priced alike, and they do not last alike either.
  3. Layers coming offOne layer of old shingle is a very different job from three.
  4. The decking underneathNobody knows what is under there until the old roof is off. This is the one that surprises people.

We survey the roof before we quote it, so the number you get is the number you pay.
