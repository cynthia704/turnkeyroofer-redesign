What a Roof Replacement Looks Like Stage by Stage

Three stages, in order. Ask to see each one on your own roof.

STAGE 01

Strip and Inspect

The old roof comes off and the decking underneath is exposed. This is where rot and hidden damage get found, and where you get shown before anything is covered.

STAGE 02

Dry-In

Underlayment goes down. This is the layer that does the work when wind lifts a shingle, and it is the reason a roof survives the next named storm.

STAGE 03

Finish and Clean

Shingle, metal, slate or tile goes on. Ventilation is set to the attic. Once payment is received, the project is completed within 24 hours.

Does the 24-Hour Guarantee Mean the Whole Roof?
The site states the roofing project is completed within 24 hours once payment is received. The estimate and the survey happen before that clock starts. Call and we will walk you through what your specific roof needs.
What Does “No Maximum Wind Speed” Actually Mean?
Most roofing wind warranties stop covering you above a stated wind speed. Ours does not carry that ceiling. The 15-Year Unlimited Wind Warranty covers named storms with no maximum wind speed.
Are You Licensed?
Yes. We are a licensed roofing contractor in Louisiana. Residential License #890459 and Commercial License #3667. Both numbers are printed in the footer of every page on this site so you can check them before you pay anyone.
