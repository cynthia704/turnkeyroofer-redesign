TurnKey Roofer · New Orleans, LA · Homepage redesign

The hero dead space is fixed. Two ways.

Thanks for the note about the empty right side of the hero. Both of the fixes that were suggested are built, so we can look at them rather than describe them.

Option 1, centered. The heading block is centered over the photo, the same way bigeasypaverpatios.com does it. The headline gets bigger, the empty right side disappears, and nothing new is added to the page.
Option 2, form in the hero. The right side carries a short estimate form instead. On a phone the form moves up directly under the headline, so it is visible without scrolling.
Still mockups, still not live. Neither form sends anything, both show a notice instead. Photography is stand-in stock from the client's own media library. There is no mobile menu yet. turnkeyroofer.com is untouched.

What to look for

  • Open both hero options on a phone. That is where they differ most.
  • Does the form belong in the hero, or does it feel pushy before anyone has read anything?
  • Does the centered version feel empty without something on the right, or does it read as calm?
  • The paver patios site has no form in its hero, for reference. This is a genuine choice, not a rule.
