What is identical in all three

  • Full-bleed hero with 4 roof photos cycling, clickable dots
  • Answer capsule under the H1, written to be quoted by AI search
  • Credential marquee, 9 badges, pauses on hover
  • Four ranked homeowner fears, each answered with a verified fact
  • Four zig-zag service rows, image side alternating
  • Roofing types gallery, 8 photo cards
  • Three replacement stages over a photo backdrop
  • Residential mosaic and a commercial band

  • Testimonial carousel, 6 real published reviews
  • Six-question FAQ, service areas, and a working estimate form
  • Sticky call bar on mobile, floating call button on desktop

What to look for when you review

  • First three seconds. Which one makes you trust a roofer you have never heard of?
  • On a phone. This market is phone first. Open it on your handset, not just a laptop.
  • The hero photo. Each direction cycles a different set of roofs. Which set fits?
  • Tone. A is urgent, B is established, D is neighbourly. Which one sounds like this company?
