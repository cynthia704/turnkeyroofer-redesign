---

## Important

- **These are mockups. Nothing here is live.** The real site is turnkeyroofer.com.
- **No invented facts.** Every licence, warranty, guarantee and testimonial shown is already
  published on the client's own site.
- **The forms do not submit.** They print a notice explaining they are a mockup.
- **Photography is stand-in**, pulled from the client's own media library. A real crew and job
  shoot is still the highest-value asset this brand could buy.
- Pages are marked `noindex` and are not intended to rank.

---

## Built with

No frameworks and no animation libraries. Plain HTML, CSS and a small amount of vanilla JavaScript.

Scroll reveals, counters, the hero photo cycle, the credential marquee and the testimonial carousel
are all hand written on top of `IntersectionObserver`. Everything honours
`prefers-reduced-motion` — with it enabled, all content still renders, motion simply stops.

Chosen deliberately: the audience is storm traffic on phones, often on poor signal after weather.
