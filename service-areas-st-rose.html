Local weather

What Wears Down St. Rose Roofs

From torrential rain to hot summers, a roof here is always exposed to the elements. These four do the most damage.

  1. High WindsStrong winds can lift or tear off shingles, leaving the roof open to water and debris.
  2. HailstormsHail can dent metal roofs and crack, fracture or puncture shingles.
  3. Rain and HumidityHeavy rain and high humidity grow mold and mildew that damage shingles and the structure below.
  4. Sun ExposureLong exposure to UV rays can fade and break down roofing materials over time.

About St. Rose

St. Rose is a community in St. Charles Parish along the Mississippi River, just west of New Orleans. French settlers first came to the area in the 1700s.

  • ParishSt. Charles Parish
  • LocationOn the Mississippi, west of New Orleans

Things to Do Near St. Rose

  • Destrehan PlantationA historic plantation home next door, open for guided tours.
  • Bonnet Carré SpillwayFishing, birdwatching and picnics near the river.

Interesting Facts

  1. St. Rose sits on the east bank of the Mississippi, between Kenner and Destrehan.
  2. Louis Armstrong New Orleans International Airport is just a few minutes away.

Getting Around

Most trips here are by car. Interstates 10 and 310 run close by, and the airport in Kenner is minutes away.

  • NearbyKenner, River Ridge, LaPlace and New Orleans

Are Roof Repairs Covered by Homeowners Insurance?
It depends on your policy. If the damage came from a covered event like a fire or a storm, the repairs may be covered. Check your policy’s specific terms.
Can a New Roof Go Over My Existing One?
Sometimes. A roofing professional should check the condition of the current roof first to see whether it’s an option.
Should the Old Shingles Come Off First?
In most cases, yes. Removing the old shingles allows a thorough inspection of the roof deck and a proper installation.
When Is the Best Time to Replace a Roof in St. Rose?
Usually spring or fall, when temperatures are moderate and the weather is more stable. A damaged roof should be replaced whatever the season.
